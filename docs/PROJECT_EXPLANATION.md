# Baali-Patro: A Simple Explanation

**What is Baali-Patro?**
Baali-Patro (which translates to "Crop Calendar") is a digital farming assistant built specifically for farmers in the Terai region of Nepal. 

**The Problem**
Farmers have generations of traditional knowledge about *when* to plant and *how* to grow their crops. However, climate change is making the weather highly unpredictable. For example:
*   A farmer might apply expensive fertilizer on Tuesday, only for a sudden, massive downpour on Wednesday to wash it all away. 
*   An unexpected heatwave might damage crops if the farmer wasn't warned to water the fields in time.

**The Solution**
Baali-Patro solves this by combining the traditional farming calendar with a **live, 7-day weather forecast**. 
Think of it as a smart timeline: it knows exactly what stage the crop is in (e.g., planting, flowering, or harvesting). Every single day, it automatically checks the upcoming weather. If it sees dangerous weather approaching—like extreme heat, freezing cold, or heavy rain—it generates a simple, actionable warning.

For example, it might say: *"Heavy rainfall expected this week. Please delay adding fertilizer and make sure water can drain from your fields."*

**Why is it special?**
1.  **Fully Automated:** No human needs to update it. Every morning at 5:45 AM, the system wakes up, checks the global weather satellites (via Open-Meteo), and updates the advice for the farmers.
2.  **Bilingual:** The entire app is available in both English and Nepali, making it accessible to local communities.
3.  **Cost-Effective:** It is built using modern "serverless" technology, which means it costs absolutely nothing (zero dollars) for the NGO or developers to keep it running day and night.
